# 32-column VIEW (2026-09-16)

When does VIEW go from 16 columns to 32 columns with teacher-exact distances and WTA readout?

On a new teacher delta and a new stitch, not a host upsample of 16 rays. `--scale 32` already fattened the same 16×18 indices. That is pixel size, not column count. v1 measured 8,086 neurons for 32-col on the Phase 3 stitch and missed `doom.v1_cap`. This job sits under `doom.v2_cap` (12,000) only if 32 distances stay bit-exact, readout stays WTA, and the stitch does not exceed the cap.

## Fan (named first)

`N_COLS` stays 16 on `tick()` and `tick_v2`. 32-col VIEW is `VIEW_32`: `N_COLS_32=32`, `FOV_HALF_32=16`, `CENTER_COL_32=16`. Rays are `ang-16` through `ang+15` on `N_ANG=64`. The heading gun is still one column: `column_angle(ang, CENTER_COL_32) == ang`. Paint is 32×18, 4-color WTA. `MAX_DIST` and `MARCH_LEN` are unchanged.

| Machine | Columns | e2 | Checkpoint | Play |
|---------|--------:|----|------------|------|
| v1 | 16 | statue | `snn_doom_v1.json` | default `--play` |
| v2 | 16 | walks | `snn_doom_v2.json` | `--machine v2` |
| v2.32 | 32 | walks | `snn_doom_v2_32.json` | `--machine v2_32` |

Do not edit the v1 or v2 checkpoints. `--machine v2_32` fail-closes if the stitch or `logs/tick_tape_v2_32.json` is missing. Do not silently load 16 columns.

## Teacher

`tick_v2_32` is the 32-col walker. `tick()` stays the statue. `tick_v2` stays the 16-col walker. Fire still consumes one ammo and kills whoever the heading ray visited. Sprite blob geometry is unchanged.

Teacher 32-col distances: `logs/teacher_ray_32.json`. SNN compare is `logs/ray_parity_32.json` after a stitch exists. Multi-tick play: `logs/tick_tape_v2_32.json`.

`TURN_STEP=4` and `ENEMY_STEP=1` are not this job.

## Order

1. Teacher `VIEW_32` and `tick_v2_32`. Tests green. 16-col ticks hold.
2. Teacher 32-distance log. Then SNN `ray_parity_32` and `tick_tape_v2_32`.
3. Freeze RAY before FRAME_READOUT.
4. Stitch under 12,000 (done: 9,957). Export `checkpoints/snn_doom_v2_32.json` through `require_v2_cap`. Do not overwrite v1 or v2.
5. If the stitch exceeds 12,000: write the count, do not export, do not move paint into Python.
